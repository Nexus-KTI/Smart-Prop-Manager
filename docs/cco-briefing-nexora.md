## 4. Brand split — commercial vs product (critical)

This is where CCO and product usually collide. Pre-agree the split.

### CCO / investors / partners — OK
- Nexora connects rent, people, and estate operations  
- Roadmap toward docs, access, staff, artisans  
- KTI as the company behind the product  

### Landlords / WhatsApp / in-app — never
- Integrated ecosystem / nexus / IoT / AI platform  
- End-to-end estate OS as the first sentence  
- Eradicate caretakers / replace property managers  
- Smart city / everything in one  

### Locked customer lines
- **Tagline:** Who paid. Who owes. What’s next.  
- **Team one-liner:** Landlord’s daily list for rent and chase — built to grow into full estate ops, without ecosystem pitch to users.

**Ask CCO:** “Can you sell Phase 1 with the tagline, and keep OS language for decks and estate pilots?”

| “When can sales promise access / KYC / artisans?” | “Not until that phase’s exit criteria. Soft pipeline language: ‘on the roadmap after rent truth is proven with landlords.’” |
